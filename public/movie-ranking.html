Ovidiu's Definitive Korean Movie Picks

My Top 3 Korean Movies

Bloodhounds

"Bloodhounds" is a Netflix Korean action-crime series in which a young boxer teams up with a delinquent and a moneylender to fight against a loan shark organization, with Season 2 expanding into an underground international boxing league.

Train to Busan

Korean zombie thriller in which a group of passengers on a high-speed train from Seoul to Busan fight to survive as a zombie outbreak spreads through the country.

The Outlaws

An action-comedy movie in which a tough detective, played by Don Lee, leads a 2004 police crackdown on rival Korean and Chinese-Korean gangs battling for turf in Seoul's Garibong district.
